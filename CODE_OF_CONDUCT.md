# Community guidelines

Auto Backup welcomes people of every background and experience level. Help make the project a respectful place to ask questions, report mistakes, and improve the software.

- Be considerate, constructive, and patient. Explain your reasoning and focus criticism on the work.
- Assume good intent while listening when someone explains the impact of your words.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's private information, credentials, or files.
- Do not engage in harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, or sexualized conduct.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam or repeated disruption.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project community spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. They should explain moderation decisions when appropriate and handle concerns with care.

For concerns, contact the maintainer through the contact options on the [Krissams GitHub profile](https://github.com/Krissams), or use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content. Avoid sharing private details in public issues. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
